Sheffield: The Steel City

Intro

Sheffield is a city in South Yorkshire, in the north of England. It’s known as the “Steel City” because of its long history of making steel and cutlery, and it’s said to be built on seven hills. It’s a friendly, down-to-earth city with a mix of industrial heritage, green spaces and a big student population.

A city with steel in its history

Sheffield’s reputation comes from its metalworking. In 1913, Harry Brearley, working in the city, developed stainless steel, which changed how we make everything from kitchen knives to surgical tools. Today, you can learn more about this past at Kelham Island, a former industrial district that now has a museum, cafés and restaurants.

Places to Visit in Sheffield

  • The Winter Garden: a large glasshouse in the city centre, full of plants from around the world. It’s free to enter, which makes it a good place to relax.
  • The Botanical Garden:Victorian gardens with glasshouses, lawns and flower beds.
  • The Peak District:right next to the city, with hills, walking trails and views. Part of Sheffield actually lies inside the Peak District National Park.

How to Get to the Peak District from the City Centre

  1. Choose your destination.
  2. Check the bus or train times the day before.
  3. Pack water, a waterproof jacket and sturdy shoes.
  4. Travel out of the city.
  5. Follow the marked trail and head back before it gets dark.
